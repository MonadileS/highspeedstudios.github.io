Information Collection and Use

To work, the apps ask you to enter connection details yourself: server addresses, ports, and in some cases credentials or configuration files. These are stored on your device and encrypted at rest using AES. If you use the optional subscription or configuration import, the source URL and content are fetched from the URL you supply. VPN and Tunneling

The apps use the Android VPN service to route your traffic through servers or proxies you configure. The apps are clients only: we do not operate those servers and we do not decide where your traffic goes. We do not intercept, read, store, or sell the content of your traffic, and we do not keep browsing history. Traffic flows only between your device and the server you choose.

While a tunnel is active, Android runs an active VPN service. The app offers on-device features: choosing which installed apps use the tunnel (requires reading the installed-apps list), importing or exporting config files you pick, and an optional app lock stored only as a salted hash on your device.

Log Data

The apps show a connection log so you can diagnose failures. It may contain the server address you entered, timestamps, and engine messages. It stays on your device only and is not transmitted to us. We do not embed any crash-reporting or analytics SDK of our own.

Cookies

Our apps are not web browsers and set no cookies of their own. Ads are rendered by the Google Mobile Ads SDK, which may use cookies or similar identifiers. Those identifiers belong to that SDK; they are controlled in Android Settings (Google → Ads), where you can reset your advertising ID. The ads themselves cannot be switched off because the apps are free and ad-supported — what you can decide is whether they are personalized.

Advertising

Our apps are supported by Google AdMob. That SDK collects and shares data (device identifiers such as the advertising ID and app set ID, approximate location from IP, app interactions, and performance diagnostics) to deliver, measure, and protect ads. We never receive a copy of that data. Precise location is never requested: the app does not use location for advertising or tracking. In the EEA, UK, and Switzerland a consent form is shown before personalized ads; app settings include an "Ad privacy options" entry in those regions.
