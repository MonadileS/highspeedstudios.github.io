## Full Description (SEO + AEO optimized)

Custom Tunnel — AIO Tunnel VPN is the all-in-one tunnel client for Android. One app, every protocol: SSH, VMess, VLESS, REALITY, Trojan, Shadowsocks, WireGuard, OpenVPN, Hysteria2, SlowDNS, and Psiphon. Connect to your own server or any provider and route your whole device through a real VPN tunnel.

Why choose Custom Tunnel?
- Full-device TUN routing via hev-socks5-tunnel — not just app proxy.
- Real-time traffic statistics: upload, download, and per-app usage.
- Split tunneling: pick exactly which installed apps use the tunnel.
- App lock with PIN or biometrics, stored only as a salted hash on your device.
- Import and export encrypted configs; scan QR codes; add subscription URLs.
- Auto-connect on untrusted Wi-Fi and on boot; schedule connections.
- Home-screen widget and quick-settings tile for one-tap connect.
- No account required. Your server details stay encrypted on your device.

How do I use Custom Tunnel?
Add your server address, port, and credentials (or import a config / scan a QR code), choose the protocol, and tap connect. Custom Tunnel establishes a system VPN and routes traffic for the apps you select. To stop, open the quick-settings tile or tap disconnect in the app.

Which protocols does Custom Tunnel support?
SSH, VMess, VLESS (with REALITY), Trojan, Shadowsocks, WireGuard, OpenVPN, Hysteria2, SlowDNS, and Psiphon. Xray and Hysteria native cores are bundled for maximum compatibility.

Is Custom Tunnel a VPN?
Yes. Custom Tunnel uses the Android VpnService to create a real VPN interface and forwards device traffic through the server you configure. We do not operate servers and do not log your browsing.

Is my data private?
Connection details are stored encrypted at rest on your device. We do not sell user data, run accounts, or keep browsing history. The app is ad-supported via Google AdMob; ad identifiers are handled by Google and can be reset in Android Settings.
