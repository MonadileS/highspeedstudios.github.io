## Full Description

Custom Tunnel is a secure VPN client that lets you connect your Android device to your own private server.

Bring your own server – Custom Tunnel does not provide VPN servers. You add your server details (address, port and credentials) or import a configuration file or QR code from your provider.

**Core functionality**
- Creates a system VPN connection using Android VpnService, routing your device traffic through the server you configure.
- Per-app routing: choose which installed apps use the VPN and which bypass it.
- Real-time connection statistics including upload, download and connection time.

**Key features**
- Support for common VPN and tunnel protocols so you can use your existing server without switching apps.
- Import and export encrypted configurations, scan QR codes, and subscribe to configuration updates.
- Auto-connect on untrusted Wi-Fi and on device startup, with optional scheduling.
- Home-screen widget and Quick Settings tile for one-tap connect and disconnect.
- App lock with PIN or biometrics. Your PIN is stored only as a salted hash on your device.
- All server credentials are stored encrypted on your device. No account is required.

**How to use**
1. Add a profile with your server address, port and credentials, or import a file or QR code.
2. Select the profile and tap Connect. Android will ask you to approve the VPN connection once.
3. To disconnect, tap Disconnect in the app or use the Quick Settings tile.

**Privacy**
Connection details are stored encrypted on your device. We do not operate VPN servers, we do not see your browsing, and we do not sell personal data. The app shows ads via Google AdMob. Advertising IDs are managed by Google Play services and can be reset in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads.

**Requirements**
You need access to a compatible server or subscription from a VPN provider. Without your own server details the app cannot establish a connection.
