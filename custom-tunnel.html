Frequently Asked Questions

Is Custom Tunnel free to use on Android?

Yes, Custom Tunnel is free to download on Google Play with core features fully accessible. It is ad-supported via Google AdMob.

Does this app collect personal data or browsing logs?

No. We do not operate VPN servers, keep browsing history, or sell data. Connection details are stored encrypted on your device only. Ads are handled by Google and can be reset in Android Settings.

What Android version is required?

Custom Tunnel supports Android 7.0 (API level 24) and above.

Does Custom Tunnel provide VPN servers?

No. Bring your own server or subscription. Without your own details the app shows an empty state and cannot connect — this is expected.

Why does Android ask to approve the VPN?

Android requires user consent before any app creates a VPN. Approve once and the tunnel starts. You can revoke it anytime in Android Settings → VPN.
